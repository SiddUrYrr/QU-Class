These terms apply to your use of ClassQU, a student attendance tracking and notification platform. By creating an account or using the service, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use ClassQU.

ClassQU is an independent project. It is not an official QUMS or university service, and it is not operated by or on behalf of the portal it reads data from.

1. Acceptable use

  • Use ClassQU only with your own QUMS account and your own Telegram account.
  • Use the service for personal attendance tracking and notifications.
  • Do not attempt to access another user's account, data or Telegram chat.
  • Do not interfere with the service, overload it or try to bypass its rate limits.

2. Account responsibility

  • You are responsible for keeping your ClassQU password secure and for activity carried out through your account.
  • You must provide an email address you control; password reset depends on it.
  • If you suspect misuse, reset your password and disconnect Telegram.

3. QUMS credentials

  • Connecting QUMS requires your QID and QUMS password. ClassQU stores the password encrypted only so it can reconnect when the portal session expires.
  • You confirm you are authorised to use the QUMS account you connect.
  • ClassQU never displays your QUMS password back to you or to anyone else.
  • You can remove the saved credentials at any time with the QUMS reset control on the setup page.

4. Telegram integration

  • Notifications are delivered through the Telegram Bot API to the chat you link.
  • One Telegram chat can be linked to one ClassQU account; linking a chat to a new account unbinds it from the previous one.
  • You can disconnect Telegram at any time, after which no further messages are sent to that chat.
  • Telegram is a third-party service with its own terms, and ClassQU cannot control its availability or delivery behaviour.

5. Notification limitations

  • Notifications depend on data being published on the QUMS portal first. ClassQU cannot report a mark that has not been entered.
  • Delivery depends on the Telegram service and on your Telegram notification settings.
  • Checks run on a schedule and at intervals, so a change can reach you some time after it happens.
  • Notifications are informational. They are not an official statement of your attendance.

6. Service availability

  • ClassQU is provided on a best-effort basis. It may be unavailable during maintenance, restarts, hosting outages or portal changes.
  • The portal may change its pages or login flow at any time, which can break parts of the integration until they are updated.
  • Free or low-cost hosting can suspend or sleep services, so continuous availability is not guaranteed.

7. Third-party services

ClassQU depends on services it does not control: the QUMS portal (the data source), native email/password authentication (sign-in and password resets), the Telegram Bot API (message delivery) and the hosting provider running this deployment. Your use of those services is also governed by their own terms, and ClassQU is not responsible for their behaviour, downtime or policy changes.

8. Your responsibility for academic information

ClassQU is a convenience layer, not an official record. You remain responsible for verifying your attendance, assignment details and deadlines with the university or the portal itself, and for any decision you make — including attendance-related decisions — based on information shown or sent by ClassQU.
