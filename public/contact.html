What to include

A little context makes a problem much faster to solve.

  • Which page you were on (for example: QUMS Setup or Telegram).
  • What you expected to happen, and what actually happened.
  • The exact message you saw, if any error was displayed.
  • Whether QUMS and Telegram show as connected on your dashboard.
Please never send your QUMS password, QID, session files, session secret, encryption key or bot token. Nobody from ClassQU needs them to help you, and sharing them puts your account at risk.
