What is ClassQU?

ClassQU is a student attendance tracker. It connects with your QUMS account and monitors your academic updates, so you do not have to keep logging in to the portal to check them yourself.

🔗

Connected to your own QUMS data

You link your QUMS account once. ClassQU reads your attendance from the portal and keeps the numbers up to date, subject by subject.

📩

Updates where you already are

Attendance changes, today's classes, assignment updates and deadline reminders are delivered to your own Telegram chat with the ClassQU bot.

How it works

Four steps, once — after that ClassQU keeps working in the background.

1

Create your ClassQU account

Register with your email and a password. Account sign-in is handled by native email/password authentication.

2

Connect your QUMS account

Enter your QID and QUMS password once and solve the portal captcha. Credentials are stored encrypted.

3

Connect Telegram

Press "Connect Telegram", open the bot and press Start. That chat now belongs to your account only.

4

Receive updates automatically

Attendance changes, daily classes, assignments and deadline reminders are pushed to your chat.

Why ClassQU?

Checking the portal repeatedly is easy to forget, and attendance marks are often entered after the class. ClassQU watches for the change and tells you when it happens, instead of expecting you to keep looking.

  • Know your subject-wise attendance without opening the portal.
  • Get an alert when a teacher marks your attendance.
  • Still find out about classes marked later for an earlier date.
  • See today's classes with room and teacher before the day starts.
  • Never miss a new assignment or its last date.
  • Each account only ever receives its own data.

Common questions

A few things students ask before getting started.

What is ClassQU?

ClassQU is a student attendance tracking and notification platform. It connects to your QUMS account, monitors your attendance and academic updates, and sends personalized notifications through Telegram.

Is my QUMS password visible to anyone?

No. Your QUMS password is stored encrypted and is never shown back to you or to anyone else — not on the dashboard, and never in a Telegram message.

Will my updates be sent to someone else?

No. Notifications go only to the Telegram chat connected to your own ClassQU account. Data is kept isolated per user.

How does backdated attendance detection work?

ClassQU also reads the month register. If attendance is marked later for an earlier class date, you receive an alert showing the actual class date and the status.
